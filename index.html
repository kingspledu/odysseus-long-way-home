CHAPTER 01

Chapter 1. The Big Wood Horse

*A Greek Myth for Young Readers | Ages 8+ | Target Lexile: 210L | American English*

*Ten big adventures. One brave hero. One long trip home.*

Odysseus was a king.

He had a wife and a son.

He loved his home.

But he had to go to war.

The war went on for ten long years.

"I want to go home," he said.

One day, he had an idea.

"Let's make a big wood horse!"

The men made the horse.

Some men hid inside it.

The other men went away.

The people of Troy saw the horse.

"What a nice gift!" they said.

They took it into their city.

That night, the men came out.

They opened the city gates.

The war was over!

Odysseus was happy.

"Now I can go home!" he said.

But his long trip was just starting.

**What We Learn:** A good idea can help us solve a big problem. But we must think about how our ideas affect other people.

**Let's Talk!** Have you ever had a good idea when something was hard? What was it?

CHAPTER 02

Chapter 2. The One-Eyed Giant

Odysseus and his men came to an island.

They saw a big cave.

"Let's look inside," said Odysseus.

There was food in the cave.

But a giant lived there!

He had one big eye.

His name was Polyphemus.

"Who are you?" the giant asked.

"My name is Nobody," said Odysseus.

The giant shut the cave with a big rock.

The men could not get out.

The giant was mean and hurt some of the men.

Odysseus had a plan.

He gave the giant some wine.

Soon, the giant fell asleep.

Odysseus and his men hurt the giant's eye.

"Nobody hurt me!" the giant yelled.

No one came to help.

The men hid under big sheep.

The sheep walked out.

The men got away!

But Odysseus made a big mistake.

"My name is Odysseus!" he yelled.

The giant told his father, the sea god, to stop Odysseus.

Now the sea would be hard to cross.

**What We Learn:** It is good to be smart. But we should not brag. Bragging can get us into trouble.

**Let's Talk!** Why did Odysseus say his name was Nobody? Have you ever wanted to brag?

---

CHAPTER 03

Chapter 3. The Bag of Wind

The men sailed on the blue sea.

One day, they met Aeolus.

He was the keeper of the winds.

"I will help you," he said.

He gave Odysseus a bag.

"Bad winds are in this bag. Do not open it."

"Thank you!" said Odysseus.

The ship went fast.

Soon, they could see home!

Odysseus was tired.

He went to sleep.

His men saw the bag.

"Is there gold inside?" one man asked.

"Let's look!" said another.

They opened the bag.

WHOOSH!

The winds came out!

The ship went far away.

"Oh, no!" cried Odysseus.

Home was gone from sight.

The men felt sad.

They had to start again.

**What We Learn:** Listen to good advice. Do not let greed make you forget what matters.

**Let's Talk!** What do you do when you want to touch something that is not yours?

---

CHAPTER 04

Chapter 4. The Magic Woman

The ship came to a green island.

A woman named Circe lived there.

She had magic!

Some of the men went to her house.

"Come in!" she said.

She gave them food and a drink.

But the drink was magic.

POOF!

The men turned into pigs!

"Oink! Oink!"

Odysseus went to help.

On the way, the god Hermes met him.

"Take this magic plant," Hermes said.

"It will keep you safe."

Odysseus took the plant.

Circe gave him a magic drink.

But he did not turn into a pig!

"Please help my friends," he said.

Circe waved her hand.

POOF!

The pigs were men again!

"My hands!" one man cried.

"My feet!" said another.

They were so happy.

Odysseus smiled.

"My friends are safe!"

**What We Learn:** Good friends help each other. When someone needs help, we can be brave and kind.

**Let's Talk!** If your best friend turned into a pig, what would you do?

---

CHAPTER 05

Chapter 5. The Land of Shadows

Odysseus had a hard question.

"How can I get home?"

Circe told him what to do.

"Go to the land of the dead. Find a wise man there."

Odysseus was scared.

The land was dark and still.

He saw the spirit of a wise man.

His name was Tiresias.

"How can I get home?" asked Odysseus.

"You must be careful," said the spirit.

"Do not hurt the sun god's cows."

"I will try," said Odysseus.

Then he saw his mother.

He ran to hug her.

But she was only a spirit.

He could not hold her.

"I miss you," he said.

His mother smiled at him.

Odysseus felt sad.

But he knew he had to go on.

"I will get home," he said.

**What We Learn:** It is okay to feel sad or scared. Being brave means doing what is right, even when we are afraid.

**Let's Talk!** Who do you miss when you are away from home? What helps you feel better?

---

CHAPTER 06

Chapter 6. The Song of the Sea

The ship sailed on.

Odysseus saw a small island.

"Be careful!" he told his men.

"The Sirens live here."

The Sirens had sweet voices.

Their songs made sailors want to come near.

But the rocks near the island were not safe.

Odysseus had an idea.

"Put wax in your ears," he told his men.

"Then you will not hear the song."

"What about you?" a man asked.

"I want to hear it," said Odysseus.

"Tie me to the ship. Do not let me go."

The men tied him tight.

Soon, the Sirens began to sing.

"Come here! Come here!"

"What a pretty song!" cried Odysseus.

"Let me go!"

But the men did not let him go.

They kept sailing.

Soon, the song was gone.

"We did it!" said Odysseus.

**What We Learn:** We all want things that may not be good for us. Making a safe plan can help us make good choices.

**Let's Talk!** Have you ever wanted to play games when it was time for bed? What helped you stop?

---

CHAPTER 07

Chapter 7. Two Sea Monsters

The ship came to a narrow part of the sea.

There were two ways to go.

But both were bad!

On one side was Scylla.

She was a monster with six heads.

On the other side was Charybdis.

She made a big hole in the sea.

The hole could pull a whole ship down!

"What do we do?" asked the men.

Odysseus had to think.

"If we go near the big hole, we may all be lost."

He chose to sail near Scylla.

"Row fast!" he yelled.

The men rowed.

SPLASH!

Scylla came out!

She took six men.

The other men got away.

Odysseus felt very sad.

He had saved the ship.

But he could not save everyone.

**What We Learn:** Some choices are hard. We must think before we act and care about people who get hurt.

**Let's Talk!** Have you ever had to choose between two things you did not like? How did you choose?

---

CHAPTER 08

Chapter 8. The Sun God's Cows

The men came to a new island.

There were big, fat cows.

"Look at all those cows!" said a man.

Odysseus shook his head.

"Do not touch them."

"Why not?" asked another man.

"They are the sun god's cows," he said.

"We must leave them alone."

But the wind was bad.

The ship could not leave.

The men got very hungry.

One day, Odysseus went to sleep.

The men looked at the cows.

"We need food," they said.

They did not listen.

They ate some of the cows.

The sun god was very angry.

Soon, a big storm came.

BOOM!

A flash of light hit the ship.

The ship broke apart.

Odysseus was the only one left.

He held on to wood in the sea.

He was all alone.

**What We Learn:** Rules can keep us safe. One bad choice can hurt many people, not just us.

**Let's Talk!** Why do we have rules at school? What can happen when we do not follow them?

---

CHAPTER 09

Chapter 9. The Island of Forever

Odysseus floated on the sea.

At last, he came to an island.

A woman named Calypso lived there.

She took care of him.

She gave him food and a safe place to sleep.

"You can stay here forever," she said.

"You can live forever, too."

Odysseus looked at the sea.

He thought of his wife.

He thought of his son.

He thought of his home.

"Thank you," he said.

"But I want to go home."

"Even if you get old?" Calypso asked.

"Yes," said Odysseus.

"Even if life is hard?"

"Yes," he said again.

Calypso kept him on her island for seven years.

At last, the gods told her to let him go.

She helped him make a small boat.

Odysseus sailed away.

"Home," he said with a smile.

"I am coming home."

**What We Learn:** Big gifts are not always the best gifts. Love, family, and home can mean more than having everything we want.

**Let's Talk!** Would you rather have a new toy or spend a fun day with your family? Why?

---

CHAPTER 10

Chapter 10. Home at Last!

Odysseus came home at last!

But his house was full of men.

They wanted to marry his wife, Penelope.

They did not know Odysseus was back.

He wore old clothes.

He looked like a poor man.

No one knew him.

Then Penelope had a test.

"Who can use my husband's big bow?" she asked.

The men tried.

They pulled and pulled.

But no one could do it.

Then Odysseus took the bow.

He pulled it with ease!

He shot an arrow through twelve ax heads.

The men were shocked.

"I am Odysseus!" he said.

With help from his son, he beat the men who had taken over his home.

At last, he went to Penelope.

"Is it really you?" she asked.

"Yes," he said.

But Penelope wanted to be sure.

She asked about their bed.

Odysseus smiled.

"Our bed is made from a tree. It cannot be moved."

Penelope knew only her husband could know that.

"Odysseus!" she cried.

She ran to him.

They held each other tight.

His son came, too.

They were a family again.

Odysseus smiled.

"I am home."

And this time, he could stay.

**What We Learn:** Never give up on the people you love. A home is not just a place. It is where we feel loved.

**Let's Talk!** What is the best thing about coming home after a long day?

---

CHAPTER 11

The Big Lesson: What Makes a Hero?

Odysseus was smart.

Odysseus was brave.

But he made mistakes, too.

Sometimes, he did not listen.

Sometimes, he was too proud.

Sometimes, he felt scared.

And sometimes, he felt sad.

But he did not give up.

He learned to ask for help.

He learned to think before he acted.

And he learned what mattered most.

It was not gold.

It was not being the strongest.

It was not living forever.

It was love.

It was family.

It was home.

**You do not need to be big or strong to be a hero. You can be a hero when you help a friend, tell the truth, make a good choice, or try again.**
